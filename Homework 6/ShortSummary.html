    Overlord Summary

    Overlord is an anime about a man named Momonga who gets trapped in a virtual reality game called Yggdrasil. He takes on the persona of his in-game character, a powerful undead sorcerer, and sets out to explore the world and find other players who may also be trapped. Along the way, he encounters various challenges and makes new allies, all while trying to uncover the mystery behind his predicament.

    It's my favorite anime because... well i'm not sure as to why but it is my favorite. The story, characters, and world-building always captivate me like no other.
